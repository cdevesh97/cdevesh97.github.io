## Current projects

Work in progress as a Research Associate at the Indian School of Business, with
Prof. Shekhar Tomar (ISB), Prof. Kanika Mahajan (Ashoka University), and
Prof. Anindya Chakraborty (IIM Ahmedabad).

### Global Shocks and the Localization of Supply Chains

Uses E-Way Bill transaction data to study how Indian supply chains restructured
after the Russia–Ukraine war. Estimated with continuous difference-in-differences
(Callaway et al., 2024) and Poisson QMLE. The data show a two-phase adjustment:
import-dependent firms first strengthened ties with existing suppliers before
expanding into new ones.

### Multi-Plant Firms and Regional Business Cycle Co-movement

Examines how multi-plant firms transmit shocks across regions, using more than
one million plant-level observations covering 114,000 firms. Co-movement indices
are constructed using principal components analysis, with Monte Carlo simulation
used to quantify the contribution of multi-plant firms to regional co-movement.

## Master's thesis

### Agricultural Trade using Machine Learning in Gravity Models
*IGIDR, 2021*

Predicted bilateral trade flows for seven agricultural commodities over 1988–2016,
comparing standard gravity estimators (OLS, PPML) with machine-learning approaches
(random forests, XGBoost, LightGBM). The machine-learning models achieved higher
out-of-sample accuracy, with R² between 0.43 and 0.81.

## Earlier research projects

**Decoupling of the Real Economy and the Stock Market** — term paper analysing the
disconnect between the Indian stock market and the real economy using firm-level
and macroeconomic data.

**Democracy and Reforms (Giuliano et al.) — replication and extension** — estimated
the causal effect of democracy on trade reforms using instrumental variables and
difference-in-differences, and tested robustness to alternative democracy measures
including the Freedom House index.
