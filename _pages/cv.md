## Research experience
**Research Associate, Indian School of Business** — Oct 2024 to present <br>
*with Prof. Shekhar Tomar (ISB), Prof. Kanika Mahajan (Ashoka University), and Prof. Anindya Chakraborty (IIM Ahmedabad)*

* *Global Shocks and Localization of Supply Chains.* Analysed E-Way Bill data to study
  supply-chain restructuring after the Russia–Ukraine war; implemented continuous
  difference-in-differences (Callaway et al., 2024) and Poisson QMLE models. Built network
  visualisations and geo-spatial mappings, documenting a two-phase adjustment in which
  import-dependent firms first strengthened existing supplier ties before expanding networks.
* *Multi-Plant Firms and Regional Business Cycle Co-movement.* Analysed over one million
  plant-level observations across 114,000 firms; constructed co-movement indices using PCA
  and Monte Carlo simulation to quantify the role of multi-plant firms in propagating
  regional shocks.

**Academic research projects, IGIDR** — 2019–21

* *Master's thesis: Agricultural Trade using Machine Learning in Gravity Models.* Predicted
  bilateral trade flows for seven commodities (1988–2016); compared econometric models
  (OLS, PPML) with machine-learning approaches (random forests, XGBoost, LightGBM).
  Machine learning achieved higher out-of-sample accuracy (R² of 0.43–0.81).
* *Term paper: Decoupling of the Real Economy and the Stock Market.* Analysed the disconnect
  between the Indian stock market and the real economy using firm-level and macro data.
* *Replication and extension: Democracy and Reforms (Giuliano et al.).* Estimated the causal
  effect of democracy on trade reforms using IV and difference-in-differences; explored
  alternative democracy measures including the Freedom House index.
