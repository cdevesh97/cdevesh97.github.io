I am a Research Associate at the [Indian School of Business](https://www.isb.edu/), working with Prof. Shekhar Tomar (ISB), Prof. Kanika Mahajan (Ashoka University), and Prof. Anindya Chakraborty (IIM Ahmedabad).

My research is in **international trade, development, and macroeconomics**, with a focus on how firms and production networks adjust to large shocks. My current projects use administrative firm- and plant-level data from India — E-Way Bill transaction records and multi-plant firm panels — to study how supply chains reorganise after trade disruptions and how shocks propagate across regions.

Before joining ISB I worked for three years in payments analytics and financial forecasting at American Express and Citibank, building econometric and machine-learning models on large transaction datasets. I hold an M.Sc. in Economics from the [Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research](https://www.igidr.ac.in/) and a B.A. in Economics from Hansraj College, University of Delhi, and I have completed PhD-level coursework in econometrics and mathematics at ISB.
