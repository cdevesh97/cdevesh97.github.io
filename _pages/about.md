## Welcome!

I am a Research Associate at the [Indian School of Business](https://www.isb.edu/), working with Prof. Shekhar Tomar (ISB), Prof. Kanika Mahajan (Ashoka University) and Prof. Anindya Chakraborty (IIM Ahmedabad).

I received my M.Sc. in Economics from the [Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research](https://www.igidr.ac.in/) in 2021, prior to which I graduated with a B.A. in Economics from Hansraj College, University of Delhi in 2018. Before joining ISB, I worked in payments analytics and financial forecasting at American Express and Citibank.

My CV is [here](/files/Devesh_CV.pdf). Please email me at [cdeveshkumar@gmail.com](mailto:cdeveshkumar@gmail.com).

**Primary Fields:** International Trade, Development Economics, Macroeconomics
